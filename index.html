The old way vs the new way

Why traditional DR breaks under pressure.

A recovery plan on paper depends on people, phone calls and memory at the worst possible moment. Control-M turns the plan into an orchestrated, repeatable workflow.

Traditional DR
With Control-M
Detection: someone has to notice the outage, then phone the on-call engineer.
Detection: failures are detected as they happen and DR is triggered automatically.
Execution: teams work from a Word or PDF runbook, step by step.
Execution: a versioned, executable runbook runs end to end.
Dependencies: order depends on who remembers it, and mistakes mean rework.
Dependencies: modelled in the workflow, each tier health-checked before the next.
People: relies on key individuals being reachable at 3 a.m.
People: failover starts without anyone having to be reachable.
Documentation: procedures drift out of date as the estate changes.
Documentation: the workflow is the documentation, so it stays current.
Testing: DR tests are rare, disruptive and labour-intensive.
Testing: rehearse on demand with repeatable runs.
Audit: evidence is pieced together from emails and chats afterwards.
Audit: every step is logged automatically with timestamps.
Validation: business checks done by phone and email.
Validation: services validated with automated checks before sign-off.
